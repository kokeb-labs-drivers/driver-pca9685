◆ I2cFailure

enum class pca9685::I2cFailure : uint8_t
strong

Why a Write() or WriteRead() hook returned false, as the optional GetLastFailure() hook reports it.

Only AddressNack changes what the driver returns: nothing acknowledged the address byte, so nothing reached the chip, and the driver returns Error::NoDevice and keeps its copy. Every other failure, and every failure of a port without the hook, is Error::Bus, and the bytes the chip acknowledged before it may have been stored.

Enumerator
Other 

Arbitration lost, a stuck line, a timeout, or a NACK the port cannot place.

AddressNack 

No device acknowledged the address byte.

DataNack 

The device acknowledged its address, then not a data byte.
