Detailed Description

Everything EnsureInitialized() writes to the chip.

The defaults are the reset values of MODE1, MODE2 and PRE_SCALE (tables 5, 6 and 8): 200 Hz from the internal oscillator, totem-pole outputs, not inverted. Fields with a unit carry it as a suffix.

The driver chooses MODE2.OUTNE itself, so that a high OE pin always sets every output to the level of a channel that is off: low when not inverted, high (totem pole) or high impedance (open drain) when inverted (table 11). The driver keeps MODE1.ALLCALL and SUB1 to SUB3 cleared, so no group address can change the chip behind its back. It keeps MODE2.OCH = 0 (the reset value): written channel registers reach the outputs at the I2C STOP, so every byte of one transfer changes together, and the bytes before a NACK at its STOP. OCH = 1 (change on ACK) is not offered: it changes a channel once "all 4 registers" are loaded (section 7.3.2, note 3), and the data sheet leaves open which bytes count after a NACK, so a switch-off could reach the output part-written.
