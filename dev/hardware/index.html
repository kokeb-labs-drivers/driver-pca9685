Address¶

The 7-bit address is 1 A5 A4 A3 A2 A1 A0 (figure 4): 0x40 (kI2cAddress) with every pin low, up to 0x7F. The address pins have no internal pull-ups (section 7.1.1): tie each one to VDD or VSS, never leave one open.

A5 A4 A3 A2 A1 A0 Address
000000 0x40
000001 0x41
110000 0x70: not usable, see below
111111 0x7F

Every PCA9685 also acknowledges the LED All Call address, 0x70, from power-up until MODE1.ALLCALL is cleared (section 7.1.2). A chip strapped to 0x70 would share it with every other PCA9685 on the bus, so the driver refuses that address (InvalidArgument, no I/O). EnsureInitialized() clears ALLCALL and the three Sub Call enables, so once started the chip answers only its own address. The general call address 0x00 with the byte 0x06 resets every PCA9685 on the bus (section 7.6); the driver never sends it, and finds the reset it causes.

Output enable (OE)¶

OE low lets the outputs follow their channels; OE high sets every output to the MODE2.OUTNE level within 60 ns (section 7.4, table 15). The driver chooses OUTNE so that OE high always gives the level of a channel that is off:

Config Channel off at the pin OUTNE OE high at the pin
not inverted (invert_outputs = false) low 00 low
inverted, totem pole high 01 high
inverted, open drain high impedance 10 high impedance

So DisableOutputs() (OE high) is the fast switch-off: a pin write, no bus traffic, and it works asleep or with the bus down. Wire OE to a GPIO with a pull-up to VDD (10 kΩ): the outputs stay disabled until the MCU drives OE low, through MCU reset and boot. The data sheet's figure 26 shows the pull-up as needed when the MCU's OE drive is open drain. Without the GPIO, tie OE to VSS and leave out the port's GpioSet().

OE can also dim or blink every output together with an external PWM on it (section 7.4); the driver does not use that, but it does not stop it either.

EXTCLK¶

The PWM counter runs from the internal oscillator, 25 MHz typical, or from EXTCLK, DC to 50 MHz (table 15). EXTCLK must be grounded when it is not used (table 3, note 2). Select it with Config::clock = ClockSource::External and Config::oscillator_hz set to its frequency; the start-up sets MODE1.EXTCLK by the two-write sequence of table 5. EXTCLK then stays set until a power cycle or a software reset: a later start with the internal clock returns ExternalClockLatched. An external clock needs no start-up wait, and it lets several PCA9685 run in step.

The internal oscillator is specified only as 25 MHz typical, so the PWM period and every pulse width are only as accurate as it is. For servo work, measure the period on one output once and set Config::oscillator_hz to 4096 x (PRE_SCALE + 1) / period (not verified on hardware: bench step 4 in Testing and CI).

I2C bus¶

Setting Value Source
Address 0x40 to 0x7F, not 0x70 figure 4, section 7.1.2
SCL Standard (100 kHz), Fast (400 kHz) or Fast-mode Plus (1 MHz) table 15
Reads Control register write, repeated START, read (kRepeatedStart) figure 22
Bus time-out The chip resets its bus interface when SDA or SCL is held low for 25 ms or more table 15, note 1

SDA and SCL need pull-ups to the bus supply (section 8): choose them for the bus capacitance and speed by the I2C specification, UM10204 section 7 (figure 26, note 1). As a start, 4.7 kΩ suits 100 kHz and 400 kHz on a short bus; Fast-mode Plus needs stronger pull-ups, which the SDA output's 30 mA sink allows (section 2).

Reference application circuit¶

Figure 26 of the data sheet: an I2C master with SDA and SCL pull-ups, OE on a GPIO with a 10 kΩ pull-up, EXTCLK and A0 to A5 strapped, and the 16 outputs driving LED strings from 5 V or 12 V through external transistors. Its remark sets INVRT = 0, OUTDRV = 1, OUTNE = 01 for that circuit; the driver's Config{} matches it except OUTNE, which the driver makes 00 so that OE high switches the strings off rather than on.

The three ways to load an output (section 7.7, table 12, figures 13 to 15):

Load Wiring Config Off at reset
LED direct from the output LEDn to anode, cathode through a resistor to VSS (up to 25 mA sink, 10 mA source per output) Config{} (totem pole, not inverted) yes
LED from the supply VDD (5.5 V at most) through a resistor and the LED into LEDn DirectLedPreset() (open drain, inverted) no
External N-type transistor (NPN, NMOS) LEDn to the gate or base; the load from its supply to the drain Config{} yes
External P-type transistor (PNP, PMOS) LEDn to the gate or base Config{.invert_outputs = true} no
Hobby servo LEDn to the servo's signal input; the servo from its own 5 V to 6 V supply, grounds joined ServoPreset() (50 Hz, totem pole) yes, no pulses

Off at reset matters: a power-on or software reset puts every output low, not inverted, with OUTNE = 00 (tables 6, 7). For a load wired active high that is off; for one wired active low (an LED from the supply, a P-type transistor) it is on, and neither OE nor the driver can prevent it until EnsureInitialized() writes INVRT again. Prefer active-high wiring where a load must not switch on by itself. LEDs with integrated Zener diodes need open drain (section 7.3.2, note 1; section 10, question 5): with totem pole the IC overheats.

Servos: 50 Hz is a 20 ms period; 1.0 ms to 2.0 ms pulses are about 205 to 410 counts of 4.88 µs (PRE_SCALE 121 at 25 MHz). SetPulseWidthUs() converts with the programmed PRE_SCALE and Config::oscillator_hz. Power servos from their own supply, not from the PCA9685's VDD: a stall current that pulls VDD below V_POR resets the chip.

Decouple VDD with 100 nF at the pin; with many outputs switching at full current, add bulk capacitance near the chip (section 10, questions 2 and 3). A supply dip below V_POR resets the chip: every channel off and asleep, which the driver finds (Contract).
